HELP & ANSWERS

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign up to create and manage your links. When you paste a URL and select Shorten link, we'll guide you through it.

Can anyone open my short link?

Yes. Anyone with the short URL or QR code can follow the link. Only you can view and manage your saved links.

What does “Total visits” mean?

It counts how many times your short links were opened, not how many unique people visited.

Can I use my own domain, such as go.mywebsite.com?

Custom domains require domain ownership, DNS verification, HTTPS and hosting configuration. This version lets you customize the link name after the platform's domain; bringing your own domain is not active yet.
